    How We Work

    Our 5-Phase Planning Roadmap

    A transparent, collaborative journey where your family stays informed and excited at every step.

    1

    Vision & Budget

    We sit down with the honoree and parents to define themes, budget caps, padrino roles, and family priorities.

    2

    Venue & Concept

    Site walkthroughs, contract locking, 3D floral mood boards, and signature color palette selections.

    3

    Vendor Curation

    Tastings with caterers and cake designers, music consultations with DJs, and photo session planning.

    4

    Court Rehearsals

    Bilingual rehearsal of the traditional waltz, surprise dance, father-daughter dance, and run-of-show timing.

    5

    Grand Gala Day

    White-glove coordination from early morning bridal suite setup through final midnight guest farewells.
