    About Fiesta Rosa Events

    A passionate, bilingual planning studio that treats every Quinceañera and Sweet Sixteen like our own family’s cherished milestone.

    Meet With Our Planners View Our Celebrations
    Founded in 2012

    From One Family Quinceañera to 500+ Unforgettable Milestones

    Fiesta Rosa began in 2012 when founder Camila Reyes planned her niece’s Quinceañera in Coral Gables and realized how rarely families find a planning studio that honors deep cultural traditions while delivering contemporary luxury design.

    Over the past fourteen years, our studio has grown into a multidisciplinary team of event architects, floral couture stylists, waltz choreography coaches, and bilingual production directors dedicated exclusively to Quinceañera and Sweet Sixteen celebrations.

    Whether we are coordinating a cathedral blessing with 14 damas and chambelanes or transforming a modern glass atrium into a Rose Gold Sweet Sixteen lounge, our goal remains unchanged: giving parents complete peace of mind while making the guest of honor feel like royalty.

    ✨ Our Mission & Core Values

    To make milestone celebrations joyful, deeply personal, and completely stress-free—empowering young women to shine and allowing families to savor every embrace.

    • ✓ Cultural Reverence: Honoring every family tradition in English & Español.
    • ✓ Budget Transparency: Real-time tracking in your family dashboard with zero hidden markups.
    • ✓ Couture Craftsmanship: Bespoke floral installations, custom stages, and concert-grade lighting.
    • ✓ Curated Vendors: 120+ insured, five-star regional celebration partners.
